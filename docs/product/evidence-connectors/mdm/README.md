## What round 2 found (the ten that change the plan)

1. **The channel caps the evidence, not the vendor.** Apple User Enrolment and Android personal work
   profiles hide serial numbers and personal apps by design and cannot be fully wiped. Every check is judged
   per platform and channel (01 1.5).
2. **Screen lock is policy evidence on every platform** (except Shared iPad): no platform reports the live
   idle time. Apple's DDM passcode status reads "compliant" with no policy at all, so compliance counts only
   with a policy installed.
3. **An erase reply never proves a wipe on Apple devices.** A Mac can reply Error and still erase, or
   Acknowledged and not erase. Wipe confirmation needs a later signal.
4. **Purchase programs give the population an MDM cannot**: Apple Business (read only API), Autopilot,
   Knox and ChromeOS pre provisioning list bought devices, so unenrolled company laptops become visible. A new
   check, `devices.purchased_devices_enrolled`, uses them. Zero touch has no read only credential.
5. **Read only does not always mean no secrets.** Fleet Observer, SimpleMDM keys, Workspace ONE, ManageEngine
   and JumpCloud can reach recovery keys or passwords on some routes. The collector keeps an endpoint and field
   allowlist per provider and a test proves no secret is stored. Hexnode has no read only key at all.
6. **Intune is richer than round 1 found**: firewall and lock per device without the export job, 60 days of
   history through the Data Warehouse, Autopilot and Entra devices as second populations, wipe history with no
   new permission.
7. **JumpCloud belongs in wave 1**: six competitors integrate it, Drata and Secureframe read Linux posture
   through it, and it is also a directory.
8. **Competitors barely check phones** (most list them without checks), **and none reads Google endpoint
   data for Mac and Windows.** Both are openings for Verity, with company owned phones only and a 2 minute
   mobile lock default (CIS 4.3).
9. **Auditors want a population, a sample and timestamped exports with the query shown.** Verity keeps daily
   state per device for the whole period and puts the query, filters and counts in every evidence file.
10. **Competitors trust the MDM's own compliance verdict.** Verity reads the settings and judges them against
    the control's thresholds, keeping the verdict as secondary evidence.
