SOC 2 is the framework library that ships with Verity today. More libraries are planned, covering international standards and the regional and sector frameworks that banks and other regulated organisations work to. This page describes how they will work and what you can do in the meantime.

## Map once, satisfy many

Your workspace runs one set of controls. A framework library will be a view over those controls: each of its requirements will point at the controls that meet it.

- A new framework will reuse every control it shares with the frameworks you already work to, and add controls only for what is new.
- Evidence will stay attached to controls, so proof you collected once will count for every framework its control maps to.
- Before you take on a framework, you will see which of its requirements your controls already cover, which they cover in part, and which new controls you would adopt.
- A requirement will show as met only when every control mapped to it is in scope, implemented and passing. Partial coverage will never show as met.
- Anything Verity suggests about one framework from another will be a draft that a person confirms.

<Callout type="note" title="Verity prepares the work, an assessor certifies it">

Certification or attestation is issued by an auditor or certification body, never by Verity. Verity prepares the work: the controls, evidence, policies and records an assessor will ask to see.

</Callout>

## What to do today

- **Work the SOC 2 library.** Give controls owners, attach evidence and track readiness. See [Controls](/docs/compliance/controls/) and [Frameworks and readiness](/docs/compliance/frameworks/).
- **Add your own controls** for requirements SOC 2 does not cover, such as a regulator's rule or a customer contract. Use **New control** in the controls library and attach evidence to it like any other control. See [Add a control of your own](/docs/compliance/controls/#add-a-control-of-your-own).
- **Map your policies to controls** so they stand as evidence. See [Documents and policies](/docs/policies/documents-and-policies/).

Because evidence attaches to controls rather than to a framework, the work you put into your controls today is the work a future library will map onto.
