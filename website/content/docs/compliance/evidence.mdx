A control that nobody can prove is an opinion. This page shows you how to add evidence, attach it to the controls it supports, review it and keep it fresh.

## What evidence is for

Evidence is the artefact that turns "we do reviews every quarter" into something an auditor can open: the exported policy, the signed review, the screenshot of the setting, the ticket that shows the work happened.

Each row is one artefact: its title, its type, the controls it supports, who owns it, its renewal date, its freshness and its review state.

**Freshness** is the column people miss. Evidence expires. A password policy export from two years ago proves nothing about today, so every piece of evidence carries a renewal date and the register shows you what has gone stale.

| Freshness | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Current | Inside its validity period |
| Aging | Within 30 days of its renewal date |
| Stale | Past its renewal date |
| No expiry | No renewal date applies |

## Choose a file or a link

There are two kinds of evidence, and the difference matters.

- **A file** is a copy Verity holds: a PDF, an export, a screenshot. It cannot change underneath you, which is exactly what an auditor wants.
- **A link** points at something that lives elsewhere: a dashboard, a ticket, a shared drive. It stays current on its own, but whoever reads it later needs access to that system.

## Attach evidence to a control

<Steps>
<Step title="Start from Evidence or from the control">

Go to **Evidence** and choose **Add evidence**. Or open the control and choose **Add evidence** on its **Evidence** tab, so the control is already filled in.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose File or Link">

Choose whether you are uploading a file or recording a link.

</Step>
<Step title="Give it a clear title">

Use a title a stranger would understand. "AWS IAM password policy export, September" beats "screenshot 3".

</Step>
<Step title="Pick the evidence type">

The **Evidence type** sets a sensible default validity: a configuration export is good for 90 days, a policy document for a year, a log export for 30 days. Change the **Validity period** if this item needs a different renewal date.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the owner">

The **Owner** is the person who will be asked to refresh it.

</Step>
<Step title="Attach it to every control it supports">

Under **Controls**, pick the controls it supports. One artefact usually proves several controls, and attaching it once to all of them is correct.

</Step>
<Step title="Save">

Choose **Add evidence**.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Use the mapping suggestions

Verity can propose which controls a piece of evidence supports.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the evidence record">

Open the item from the register.

</Step>
<Step title="Ask for suggestions">

On the **Controls** tab, choose **Suggest mappings**.

</Step>
<Step title="Read the proposals">

You get a list of proposed controls with a reason for each. Nothing is attached yet.

</Step>
<Step title="Keep the right ones">

Choose **Link** on the ones that are right and **Dismiss** the rest.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="A suggestion is a draft, always">

Nothing Verity proposes is attached to a control until a person approves it, and the approval is recorded with your name against it. That rule holds everywhere in the product.

</Callout>

## Review evidence

Evidence gets reviewed so somebody other than the person who uploaded it agrees it proves what it claims. Review starts once the evidence is linked to a control.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the evidence record">

Set the register's **Review** filter to **Pending review** and open the item.

</Step>
<Step title="Check what it proves">

Read what is attached and which controls it is said to support.

</Step>
<Step title="Record your decision">

In the **Review** panel, choose **Approve**, or choose **Reject** and say what needs fixing.

</Step>
</Steps>

A rejection is not a delete. The artefact stays with the reason attached, so the next person can see it was considered and why it was not good enough.

## Keep evidence fresh

<Steps>
<Step title="Find what is expiring">

Filter the register by **Freshness** to find what is **Stale** or **Aging**.

</Step>
<Step title="Go to the owner">

Each expiring item has an owner. That is who collects the new copy.

</Step>
<Step title="Add the new copy and keep the old one">

Attach the new artefact with a new renewal date. Keep the old one: it proves the control was operating in the earlier window, which is exactly what a Type II audit examines.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Evidence that arrives without uploading

- **Connections.** A connected system runs checks and attaches their output to the controls those checks map to. See [Automated checks](/docs/compliance/automated-checks/).
- **Other modules.** A published policy, a completed task and a vendor's SOC 2 report can all act as evidence for the control they serve.
