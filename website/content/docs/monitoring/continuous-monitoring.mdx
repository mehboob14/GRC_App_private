Continuous monitoring will turn your connected systems into a standing watch on your controls. This page describes what is planned, and what you can already use today.

## What it will do

Checks will run on a schedule across every system you connect, and every run will be kept, so each control shows its history across your audit window rather than a single snapshot.

- **Findings for every failure.** A failing check will open a finding for the specific resource it failed on. A check that fails on three repositories will open three findings.
- **Automatic resolution.** When the check passes again, its finding will resolve itself. Nobody has to remember to close it.
- **Alerts.** A control that moves to failing will raise an alert, so the people responsible hear about it without waiting for the next review.
- **Waivers.** When a failure is accepted for now, a waiver will record it. Waivers will be time-boxed, justified and approved by someone other than the person who asked. When a waiver expires, the finding will reopen.
- **Scoping and exclusions.** You will be able to scope checks to the repositories, accounts or tags that matter. Excluding a resource will need a reason, will be recorded in the audit log, and will be printed on the evidence, so an auditor can see what was left out and why.

## Error is never fail

A check that cannot read a system, because a token expired or permissions were reduced, will record an error, not a failure. A broken connection will never look like a failed control. You will see that the check could not run, and why, rather than a red mark against a control that may be working perfectly well.

## What you can use today

GitHub checks already run daily. Connect GitHub and seven checks read your repositories every day and attach their results to the controls they support, as dated evidence. See [Connect GitHub](/docs/integrations/github/) and [Automated checks](/docs/compliance/automated-checks/).

Until findings and alerts arrive, the latest run's failing results are your open issues. Read them on a control's **Automation** tab, and raise a [task](/docs/tasks/overview/) to track the fix. For systems Verity cannot connect to yet, keep your controls proven with [evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/) collected by hand.
