Everything in Verity eventually becomes work somebody has to do: collect this evidence, review that access list, fix the finding, update the policy. **Tasks** is where that work is assigned, tracked and closed. This page shows you how to raise, work and close a task.

<Screenshot name="tasks-register" alt="The tasks and issues register, with filters for kind, status, priority, category and assignee, and a selected task's summary open beside the list" />

## Tasks and issues

Two kinds share one register. Use **All**, **Tasks** or **Issues** above the list to choose which you see.

- A **task** is planned work. It has an owner and a due date.
- An **issue** is something that went wrong. It carries a severity, and it can hold corrective and preventive actions underneath it.

The difference matters for an auditor: issues show that when something broke, you noticed, acted, and can show the actions you took. See [Corrective actions on an issue](/docs/tasks/service-levels-and-approvals/#corrective-actions-on-an-issue).

## Raise and assign a task

<Steps>
<Step title="Start a new task">

Go to **Tasks**, choose **New task**, then choose **Task**. To record something that went wrong, choose **Issue** instead.

</Step>
<Step title="Say what done looks like">

Give it a title that says what done looks like: "Collect the Q3 access review sign-off from IT" rather than "access review".

</Step>
<Step title="Choose a category and priority">

Choose a **Category** (security, privacy, operations, data, regulatory, vendor or other) and a **Priority**.

</Step>
<Step title="Set the owner and assignees">

Set the **Owner**, who is accountable, and the **Assignees**, who do the work. They are often the same person.

</Step>
<Step title="Set a due date">

Set a **Due date**. If the work falls under a service level, choose its **SLA level** too. See [Service levels and approvals](/docs/tasks/service-levels-and-approvals/).

</Step>
<Step title="Create it">

Choose **Create**. The assignees are notified.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Work and close a task

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the task">

Open the task from the register or from your notifications.

</Step>
<Step title="Start it">

Choose **Start** in the header to move it to **In progress**. Other moves, such as **Block** and **Cancel**, are in the **Actions** menu.

</Step>
<Step title="Keep the conversation in one place">

Use the **Comments** tab. Comments are part of the record.

</Step>
<Step title="Send it for review">

When the work is done, choose **Send for review** to move it to **Under review**.

</Step>
<Step title="Close it">

Once the work has been checked, choose **Close** and record how it was resolved. The note goes on the permanent history.

</Step>
</Steps>

Every transition is recorded with the person and time on the **Activity** tab. Cancelling also asks for a reason, and the record is kept: nothing is deleted.

If the work produced proof for a control, add it as [evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/) and attach it to that control.

## Break work down and see what it relates to

- **Sub-tasks** break a large task into steps that can be assigned separately. On the **Sub-tasks** tab, type a title and press <Kbd>Enter</Kbd>. Each sub-task opens as a task of its own.
- **Related** shows what this task is attached to: the control, the finding, the vendor or the document that caused it. It also shows whether the task was raised by hand or promoted from a corrective action.

For service levels, approvals and corrective actions, see [Service levels and approvals](/docs/tasks/service-levels-and-approvals/).
