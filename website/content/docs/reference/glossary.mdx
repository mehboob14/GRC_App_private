Words this platform uses, and what they mean here. Where a word has a general industry meaning and a narrower Verity meaning, both are given. For how these ideas fit together, see [Key concepts](/docs/get-started/key-concepts/).

## A

### Acceptance

A recorded decision to tolerate a risk or a finding rather than fix it. Always has a justification, an approver who is not the requester, and an expiry date. When the expiry passes, the thing reopens on its own. See [Treat or accept a risk](/docs/risk/treatment-and-acceptance/) and [Remediation and exceptions](/docs/vulnerabilities/remediation/#exceptions-and-acceptance).

### Acknowledgement campaign

A targeted request for named people to read and sign a published document. The record of who signed, and when, is the evidence that a policy reached its audience. See [Acknowledgement campaigns](/docs/policies/acknowledgement-campaigns/).

### Approval gate

A lifecycle stage that will not clear until specific conditions are met. The vendor approval stage is one: open findings and unfinished earlier stages block it. See [Assessment and findings](/docs/vendors/assessment-and-findings/).

### Asset

Anything you run that processes, stores or carries something you care about: an application, a server, a dataset, a cloud resource, a business service. See [Asset inventory](/docs/assets/inventory/).

### Audit log

The append only record of every state changing action. Cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including an administrator. See [Audit log and isolation](/docs/admin/audit-log-and-isolation/#the-audit-log).

## C

### CIA rating

Confidentiality, integrity and availability, each rated 1 to 5. The three inputs from which an asset's criticality is derived. See [how the tier is derived](/docs/assets/inventory/#criticality-how-the-tier-is-derived).

### Control

Something your organisation does to keep a promise. A control answers one or more framework criteria and is proven by evidence. See [Controls](/docs/compliance/controls/).

### Criterion

A single requirement in a framework, for example SOC 2 CC6.1. See [Frameworks and readiness](/docs/compliance/frameworks/).

### Criticality tier

Low, medium, high or critical. Derived for assets from the CIA rating and exposure; derived for vendor engagements from the five tiering questions. See [Asset inventory](/docs/assets/inventory/) and [Tiering](/docs/vendors/tiering/).

## D

### Definition (vulnerability)

The weakness itself, for example a CVE. Distinct from a finding, which is that weakness on one of your assets. See [Findings and priority](/docs/vulnerabilities/findings-and-priority/#definitions-and-findings).

## E

### Engagement

One thing you use a vendor for. A vendor can have several, and each is tiered and assessed separately. See [How vendor risk works](/docs/vendors/overview/).

### EPSS

Exploit Prediction Scoring System: the probability that a given vulnerability will be exploited in the wild in the next 30 days. See [Findings and priority](/docs/vulnerabilities/findings-and-priority/#priority-why-the-order-is-not-just-cvss).

### Evidence

An artefact that proves a control operates: a file Verity holds, or a link to something that lives elsewhere. See [Evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/).

## F

### Finding (vendor)

A gap identified in a third party, with a severity, an owner and a due date. See [Assessment and findings](/docs/vendors/assessment-and-findings/).

### Finding (vulnerability)

A weakness on one of your assets at one location. See [Findings and priority](/docs/vulnerabilities/findings-and-priority/).

### Framework

A published set of criteria you are being measured against, for example SOC 2. See [Frameworks and readiness](/docs/compliance/frameworks/).

### Freshness

How current a piece of evidence is, against the renewal date its type implies. See [Evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/).

## G

### Group

A team of people in the workspace, usable as an assignee, an approver or a campaign target. See [Roles and groups](/docs/admin/roles-and-groups/#groups).

## I

### Inventory hygiene

The completeness score on an asset: five checks on the record plus a freshness clock on when a person last reviewed it. See [Hygiene and dependencies](/docs/assets/hygiene-and-dependencies/#inventory-hygiene).

## K

### KEV

The Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue published by CISA. A vulnerability on it is being exploited now, and Verity floors its priority accordingly. See [Findings and priority](/docs/vulnerabilities/findings-and-priority/#priority-why-the-order-is-not-just-cvss).

## M

### Membership

A person's place in one workspace. All ownership and assignment in Verity points at a membership, never at a global user, so nobody can be attached to a workspace they do not belong to. See [People and invitations](/docs/admin/people/).

## P

### Portal (vendor)

The unauthenticated page where a vendor answers a questionnaire. Reached by a single use link that is shown once. See [Questionnaires and the portal](/docs/vendors/questionnaires/).

## R

### Register

The list view of a module: controls, risks, vendors, assets, findings, tasks, documents. See [Finding your way around](/docs/get-started/navigating-verity/).

### Residual risk

What is left after your controls act. Contrast inherent risk, which is the level before they do. See [The risk register](/docs/risk/risk-register/).

### Review cadence

How often a record must be revisited. Set per criticality for assets, per tier for vendors, per document for policies. See [Hygiene and dependencies](/docs/assets/hygiene-and-dependencies/#set-your-own-review-cadence), [Tiering](/docs/vendors/tiering/) and [Draft a policy](/docs/policies/documents-and-policies/).

## S

### Scope

What is in and out of an audit, and why. See [Frameworks and readiness](/docs/compliance/frameworks/).

### Separation of duties

The rule that the person who requests something cannot be the person who approves it. Enforced on risk acceptance and the vendor approval gate. See [Treat or accept a risk](/docs/risk/treatment-and-acceptance/) and [Assessment and findings](/docs/vendors/assessment-and-findings/).

### Service level (SLA)

A promise about how quickly work of a given priority or severity is completed. Breaches are visible and escalate. See [Service levels and approvals](/docs/tasks/service-levels-and-approvals/) and [remediation windows](/docs/vulnerabilities/remediation/#remediation-windows).

### Shadow IT

Software in use that never went through intake. Recorded as a discovered app and triaged into a vendor, a link to an existing vendor, or ignored with a reason. See [Contracts to offboarding](/docs/vendors/contracts-monitoring-offboarding/).

### Soon

A badge marking a screen that is not built yet. Nothing behind one holds real data. Pages in these docs marked as coming soon describe what is planned.

### Subprocessor

A third party your vendor relies on. Your fourth party. See [Tiering](/docs/vendors/tiering/).

## T

### Tiering

The five question assessment that decides how much diligence a vendor engagement earns. See [Tiering](/docs/vendors/tiering/).

### Trust Services Criteria

The five SOC 2 categories: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality and Privacy. See [Frameworks and readiness](/docs/compliance/frameworks/).

## W

### Workspace

One organisation's sealed area of Verity. A person may belong to several and sees one at a time. See [Audit log and isolation](/docs/admin/audit-log-and-isolation/#data-isolation).
