You rarely need to start a register from an empty page. This page covers adopting risks from the library, importing a spreadsheet, and the settings an administrator uses to shape each register. Adding risks either way needs permission to manage risks.

## Adopt risks from the library

The **Library** is a catalogue of common risks, already written and scored, that you can adopt into a register. It is faster than staring at an empty page, and you can edit anything you adopt.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the library">

Go to **Risks > Library**. Library risks are grouped by category. Search, or filter by **Category** or **Framework**. Set **Show** to **Hide added** to hide the risks already in this register.

</Step>
<Step title="Read before you adopt">

Click a risk to read its description, root cause, consequences, recommendations, suggested treatment and suggested controls.

</Step>
<Step title="Add what applies">

Tick the risks you want, then click **Add to** followed by the register's name. From an open risk, click **Add to register**.

</Step>
</Steps>

An adopted risk copies the library text and links the suggested controls your workspace has. Its score is rescaled to the register's matrix, and the card shows **In register** from then on. Library updates never rewrite a risk you have already adopted, so a risk you reviewed stays as you left it.

## Import risks from a spreadsheet

**Import** takes a spreadsheet. Download the template, fill it in, preview what Verity read, then commit. The preview is where you catch a column that mapped to the wrong field.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the import">

On **Risks**, check the register menu shows the register you want, then click **Import**.

</Step>
<Step title="Download the template">

Click **Download template**. The Excel template has drop-down lists for this register, and the subcategories follow the category.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill it in and upload it">

Fill in the template, then drop the file on the dialog or choose it. Excel and CSV files both work.

</Step>
<Step title="Check the preview">

Verity shows every row as it read it, with how many are ready and how many have errors. Rows with errors say what is wrong and are skipped. Tick **Only rows with issues** to see just those.

</Step>
<Step title="Commit">

Click the **Import** button, which shows how many risks are ready, to add them. Nothing is saved until you do.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Set up a register

**Risks > Settings** is where an administrator manages registers: the scales, the bands, the categories and the review cadence. You need permission to configure risks to change them.

<Screenshot name="risks-settings" alt="Risk settings showing a register card with its type, number of risks, matrix size and review cadence, buttons for Matrix and settings and for Categories, and further configuration marked Soon" />

Each register card shows its type, how many risks it holds, its matrix size and how often its risks are reviewed. **Open** switches the rest of the Risks screens to that register.

### Create or change a register

Click **New register**, or **Matrix and settings** on an existing register, and set:

- **Name**, **Register type** (for example Enterprise, Project or Third party), an optional **Description** and the **Owner**.
- **Review every (days)**: the review cadence for risks in this register.
- **Matrix size**: from 3 to 6 levels each for likelihood and impact.
- **Level labels**: a name for each level, and what it means, so people score consistently.
- **Severity bands**: the score at which medium, high and critical begin. A preview shows the matrix as you change it.
- **Default register**, and for any other register, **Archived** once you no longer use it.

Then click **Create register** or **Save register**.

### Edit categories

Click **Categories** on a register to add, rename or reorder categories and their subcategories. Categories still used by risks are archived instead of deleted, so nothing already filed under them loses its place.
