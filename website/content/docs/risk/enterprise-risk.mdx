Enterprise risk management will grow the risk register into a full programme: structured assessments, incidents, indicators and appetite, all feeding the registers you already keep. This page describes what is planned. The register itself is live today.

## What it will add

- **Risk and control self-assessments (RCSA).** Campaigns by period will ask control owners to rate how well their controls are designed and how well they operate, optionally against a specific risk. Results will roll up by business unit.
- **Risk assessments.** A structured assessment for a chosen scope will follow the ISO 31000 stages (scope, identify, analyse, evaluate and treat), with sign-off at each stage. Every risk identified inside an assessment will land in a register.
- **Framework assessments.** You will record maturity and gaps per requirement for frameworks such as ISO 27001, HIPAA and GDPR, on the same control engine your controls already use.
- **Incidents.** You will record an incident, classify it by severity, keep a timeline and link it to the risks and controls involved. An incident on a risk will prompt a rescore.
- **Key risk indicators (KRIs).** Named measures with thresholds will be measured over time. A breached threshold will alert the owner, and the risk will show the trend.
- **Risk appetite and tolerance.** You will set appetite and tolerance per category. Risks above appetite will be flagged, and risks above tolerance will escalate to a named owner.
- **Configurable workflows.** You will define the approval steps that decisions on risks, incidents, documents and vendors go through, instead of today's single approver for a risk acceptance.

## AI-proposed mitigations: a person decides

AI will draft treatment plans and actions from a risk, its controls and its incidents. A draft will be marked as AI-drafted and will go through the same review and approval as anything a person writes. AI will never change a risk score, accept a risk or close one. It drafts, and a person decides.

## Where you will see it

The **Assessments** and **KRIs** tabs are already visible in **Risks**, marked **Soon**, and so are risk appetite and approval workflows under **Risks > Settings**. Nothing behind a Soon badge holds real data yet.

## What you can use today

You can already keep several registers, score inherent and residual risk, link controls, track treatment actions as tasks, and accept risks with an approver and an expiry. Until incidents arrive, you can record something that went wrong as an issue in Tasks, with corrective and preventive actions.
