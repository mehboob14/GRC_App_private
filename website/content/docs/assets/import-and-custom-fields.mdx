Most teams arrive with a list already. This page shows you how to import a spreadsheet of assets, what each column expects, and how to add custom fields for anything else your organisation tracks.

## Import a spreadsheet

You need permission to bulk import assets. Admins have it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the import page">

Go to **Assets** and choose **Import**.

</Step>
<Step title="Download the template">

Click **Download template** so your columns match. The template has a header row and one worked example. You can also get it from **Export > Import template** on the register.

</Step>
<Step title="Fill it in">

Only `name` and `asset_type` are required. The table below explains the columns that most often trip people up.

</Step>
<Step title="Upload your file">

Click **Choose file** and pick your CSV or Excel file. Verity reads it and shows you what it understood, row by row, with errors called out before anything is created. Each row reads **Ready** or lists what is wrong with it.

</Step>
<Step title="Fix or commit">

Fix anything wrong in the sheet and upload again, or commit what parsed. The button carries the count, for example **Import 12 assets**, and only the rows marked ready are created.

</Step>
</Steps>

### What the columns expect

| Column | What to put in it |
|---|---|
| `name` | Required. The name your team uses for the asset. |
| `asset_type` | Required. One of `application`, `infrastructure`, `data`, `cloud`, `third_party` or `business_service`. |
| `status` | `planned`, `active` or `in_maintenance`. Leave it empty for active. |
| `confidentiality_rating`, `integrity_rating`, `availability_rating` | 1 to 5, or empty to leave the asset unassessed. |
| `data_classification` | `public`, `internal`, `confidential` or `restricted`. |
| `internet_facing`, `customer_facing` | `true`, `yes`, `y` or `1` for yes. Anything else reads as no. |
| `compliance_scope` | Several values separated by semicolons, for example `SOC2;PCI`. |
| `owner_name` and the other people columns | The person's name exactly as it appears in your workspace. A name that matches nobody is flagged on the row. |
| `criticality`, `criticality_override_reason` | Leave both empty and Verity derives the tier from the CIA ratings. Fill them in only to override the derived tier, with the reason. |

<Callout type="tip" title="Let Verity derive criticality">

Criticality comes from the CIA ratings and exposure, as described in [Asset inventory](/docs/assets/inventory/#criticality-how-the-tier-is-derived). Filling in the `criticality` column overrides that, so use it only when you have a reason a formula cannot see.

</Callout>

Custom fields are not part of the import. Fill them in on each asset afterwards, where the field definitions are shown.

Connecting a scanner to keep the inventory in step automatically is marked <Status value="soon" /> on the import page. Until then, import and edit by hand.

## Custom fields

If your organisation tracks something this inventory does not ask for, add it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open the custom fields settings">

Go to **Assets > Settings**. Under **Custom fields**, click **Add field**.

</Step>
<Step title="Name it and choose the type">

Enter the **Field name** and choose the **Type**: text, long text, number, date, choice, or yes or no. For a choice field, list the **Choices** one per line. Add **Helper text** if people need a hint; it shows under the field on the form.

</Step>
<Step title="Decide whether it is required">

Switch on **Required** if every asset must have it. It applies the next time each record is saved, not to records already stored.

</Step>
<Step title="Add it">

Click **Add field**. It appears on the form the next time somebody opens it.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot name="dialog-custom-field" alt="The Add a field dialog with Field name, Type set to Text, optional helper text and a Required switch, over the asset settings page" />

The field then appears on the asset form under "This workspace also asks", and on the asset record in the **Also recorded** panel.

You cannot change a field's type once it exists, so pick it carefully. You can still rename it, change its choices or helper text, or make it required, with **Edit**.

Fields are archived rather than deleted, so a value written last year stays readable after you stop collecting it. Use **Archive** to stop collecting a field and **Restore** to collect it again.

Findings have custom fields of their own. See [Remediation and exceptions](/docs/vulnerabilities/remediation/#custom-fields) and the overview in [Custom fields](/docs/admin/custom-fields/).
