AI in Verity will draft and suggest. It will never publish and never decide. This page describes the planned AI features, the rules every one of them follows, and the AI help you can already use.

## What it will do

- **Document drafting.** AI will draft policies, procedures, guidelines and standards for your workspace. Each draft then goes through the same review, approval and staff acknowledgement as a document a person wrote.
- **Scan report parsing.** AI will read a scan report and turn it into a reviewable set of proposed findings. A person confirms them before any finding is created.
- **Risk mitigation proposals.** AI will propose ways to treat a risk. The risk owner decides what, if anything, to use.
- **Ask Verity.** An assistant you can ask plain-language questions about your compliance programme.

## The rules AI follows

These rules are not settings you can switch off. They are how the product works.

- **AI drafts. People decide.** Nothing AI produces is published, approved or acted on without a person reviewing it.
- **AI never publishes, approves, closes or changes a score.** It never marks a control passing, never closes a finding, never approves evidence and never changes a risk score.
- **AI content is marked.** Anything AI writes is marked as an AI draft, and the mark stays with it, so you and your auditor can always tell what was machine-generated.
- **The same approval as human content.** An AI draft goes through exactly the same approval as content a person wrote. There is no lighter path.
- **Prompts and outputs are logged**, with the person and the model used, so a bad draft can always be traced.
- **You can always work manually.** If the AI provider is down, nothing stops you writing your own policy or recording your own finding.

## What you can use today

A few places already offer drafts that you accept or ignore, under the same rules:

- **AI Assist** on a new risk proposes a category, description, root cause, consequences, recommendations and an opening score. Nothing is applied until you click to use it. See [The risk register](/docs/risk/risk-register/).
- **Mapping suggestions** on a piece of evidence propose which controls it supports, with a reason for each. Nothing is attached until a person approves it. See [Evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/).
- **Remediation plans** for vulnerability findings can be drafted by AI and are then marked **AI draft**. A person still approves the plan and verifies the fix. See [Remediation and exceptions](/docs/vulnerabilities/remediation/).

For policies today, the fastest route is the template library: pick a template and you get a complete draft in your workspace's name. See [Draft a policy](/docs/policies/documents-and-policies/).
