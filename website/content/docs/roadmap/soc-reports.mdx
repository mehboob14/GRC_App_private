SOC 2 is one of three System and Organization Controls reports. Verity will help you prepare for SOC 1 and SOC 3 as well, working from the same control set you already use for SOC 2. This page explains the difference between the reports, what is planned, and what you can use today.

## Three reports, one control set

| Report | What it covers |
|---|---|
| SOC 1 | Controls relevant to your customers' financial reporting, known as internal control over financial reporting (ICFR). |
| SOC 2 | Controls against the Trust Services Criteria: Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality and Privacy. |
| SOC 3 | A general-use summary report on the same criteria as SOC 2, written so it can be shared widely. |

## What it will do

- **Prepare SOC 1 work alongside SOC 2.** You will be able to prepare for a SOC 1 examination from the controls you already operate, so the controls relevant to financial reporting are tracked in the same library, with the same owners, evidence and history.
- **Prepare a SOC 3 alongside SOC 2.** Because a SOC 3 summarises the same criteria as a SOC 2, it will draw on the same controls and evidence rather than a separate exercise.
- **Shared, not copied.** A control is not duplicated for each report. Evidence you collect once will serve every report the control supports.

## Who issues the report

The reports themselves are issued by an independent CPA firm after its own examination. Verity prepares the work the auditor examines: your controls and their owners, the evidence that each one operates, your published policies and who signed them, and the audit trail behind all of it. Verity does not issue reports.
