The Trust Center will let your workspace publish a public page that shows customers and prospects how you look after security. This page describes what is planned, and what you can already do in Verity today.

## What it will do

- **Show your security posture.** A page you publish will give customers a clear view of how you protect their data.
- **Share your policies.** You will choose which policies appear on it.
- **List your certifications and attestations.** The page will show the certifications and attestations you hold.
- **Control access to documents.** Some documents will be open to anyone, and others shared only on request, for example once a visitor has asked for access or accepted a non-disclosure agreement.

## Built on what you already keep

The Trust Center will draw on what you already keep in Verity: your published policies, your controls and the evidence behind them.

Nothing will be published without a person approving it. Someone in your organisation will decide what appears on the page and who can see each document.

## What you can do today

The records a Trust Center will draw on are the ones you already keep for an audit, and all of that work is live now.

- **Keep your policies approved and published.** A published policy has an owner, an approval record and a version history. See [Approve and publish](/docs/policies/approval-and-publishing/).
- **Show that people have read them.** [Acknowledgement campaigns](/docs/policies/acknowledgement-campaigns/) record who signed which version, and when.
- **Keep evidence fresh.** Evidence expires, and current evidence is what proves your controls work. See [Evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/).
- **Know your readiness.** The compliance dashboard shows how many controls are passing and which criteria are weak. See [Frameworks and readiness](/docs/compliance/frameworks/).
