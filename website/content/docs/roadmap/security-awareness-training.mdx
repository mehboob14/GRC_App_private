Most frameworks expect every member of staff to be trained in security awareness, and expect you to prove it. This feature will let you assign that training, see who has completed it, and keep the record as evidence. This page describes what is planned and what you can use today.

## What it will do

- **Assign training to staff.** You will be able to assign security awareness training to the people in your workspace.
- **Track completion beside acknowledgements.** Completion will sit alongside policy acknowledgements, so one place answers both "who has read the policies?" and "who has done the training?".
- **Keep completion as evidence.** Completion records will count as evidence for the controls that expect training, such as the security awareness training control in your library, so an auditor's question is answered with names and dates rather than a chase.

As with every other change in Verity, assignments and completions will be recorded in the [audit log](/docs/admin/audit-log-and-isolation/).

## What you can use today

You can already put security awareness material in front of named people and record that they read it.

<Steps>
<Step title="Publish the material as a document">

Create a document for your security awareness material, for example a guideline, and take it through approval to publication. See [Draft a policy](/docs/policies/documents-and-policies/) and [Approve and publish](/docs/policies/approval-and-publishing/).

</Step>
<Step title="Run an acknowledgement campaign">

Start an acknowledgement campaign on the published document and target the people, roles or groups who must read it. Each recipient opens the document before they can sign. See [Acknowledgement campaigns](/docs/policies/acknowledgement-campaigns/).

</Step>
<Step title="Keep the record as evidence">

The campaign's record of who signed, and when, is evidence that the material reached its audience. If you also use an external training provider, attach its completion export to the training control as [evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/).

</Step>
</Steps>

To chase the people who have not finished, raise a [task](/docs/tasks/overview/) or send a reminder from the campaign page.
