Questionnaire automation will help you answer the security questionnaires your customers send you, using what you already keep in Verity. This page describes what is planned, how it differs from the vendor questionnaires you can send today, and what to do in the meantime.

## Inbound, not outbound

Verity already handles questionnaires in one direction: you send them to your suppliers. Questionnaire automation is the reverse.

| | Vendor questionnaires (live) | Questionnaire automation (planned) |
|---|---|---|
| Who asks | You ask a vendor | Your customer asks you |
| Who answers | The vendor, through the vendor portal | Your team, from your own records |
| What it produces | A residual score, a grade and findings about the vendor | Answers about your organisation, ready to send back |

If you want to assess a supplier, you need [Questionnaires and the vendor portal](/docs/vendors/questionnaires/), which is live now.

## What it will do

- **Answer from your records.** Answers will draw on your existing controls, policies and evidence, so you describe what you actually do rather than starting from a blank page each time.
- **Suggest answers with AI.** For each question, AI will suggest an answer based on those records.
- **Keep a person in charge.** Someone on your team will review each answer, edit it where needed and approve it before anything is sent.

## AI drafts, a person decides

Verity's rule for AI applies here too: AI drafts, and humans decide. A suggested answer will be marked as AI-drafted and will go through the same review and approval as an answer a person writes. Nothing will be sent to a customer until someone approves it, and AI will never send, submit or approve anything on its own.

## What you can do today

Good answers come from good records, and keeping those records current is work you can do now.

- **Keep controls owned and implemented.** Each control has an owner and a status. See [Controls](/docs/compliance/controls/).
- **Keep policies published.** A published policy is the version people are held to. See [Draft a policy](/docs/policies/documents-and-policies/).
- **Keep evidence fresh.** Current evidence proves that a control operates. See [Evidence](/docs/compliance/evidence/).
