The administration pages are for whoever administers the workspace. Everything they cover sits under **Settings** at the bottom of the left navigation. This page shows you who has access, how to invite someone, how to give an external auditor a time-boxed window, and how to remove someone without breaking the audit trail.

## The people list

Go to **Settings > Access Management > People**. The list shows everyone with access to this workspace and what they can do: each member's role, groups, two-factor status (**MFA**) and when they were last active.

<Screenshot name="settings-people" alt="The People tab under Access Management, listing members with their role, groups, MFA status, background and documents columns and last active time, with an Invite member button" />

The **Background** and **Documents** columns read **Unknown** for now: background checks and per-person document sign-off are not tracked on this list yet. Use [acknowledgement campaigns](/docs/policies/acknowledgement-campaigns/) to see who has signed a policy.

The menu at the end of each row (⋯) holds what you can change without leaving the list: **Edit name**, **Change role**, **Add to group** and **Disable member**. A person's name is theirs across every workspace they belong to, so **Edit name** changes it everywhere.

## Invite someone

<Steps>
<Step title="Start the invitation">

Go to **Settings > Access Management > People** and click **Invite member**.

</Step>
<Step title="Say who they are">

Enter their **Full name** and **Work email**.

</Step>
<Step title="Choose how they join">

Under **Invite as**, choose **Member** for someone in your organisation and pick the **Role** they should hold. If your workspace has groups, you can put them in a **Group** now too.

</Step>
<Step title="Set an access window for an external auditor">

For an external auditor or consultant, choose **Guest auditor** and set an access window with **Access starts** and **Access ends**: the dates between which they can sign in. Outside those dates the account still exists but cannot get in.

</Step>
<Step title="Send the invitation">

Click **Create invite**. They get an email with a one-time accept link, set their own password, and enrol in two-factor authentication if your policy requires it. The link works once and expires in 7 days.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note" title="If email is not set up">

When your workspace cannot send email, nothing is sent. The dialog shows the accept link instead: use **Copy link** and pass it on yourself. It is shown only once. Outbound email is configured under [Organisation profile](/docs/admin/security-settings/#organisation-profile).

</Callout>

A guest auditor is not asked for a role in the invitation, so check the role they were given with **Change role** in the row menu, and make sure they hold only what the engagement needs. Roles are explained in [Roles and groups](/docs/admin/roles-and-groups/).

Invited people show as **Invited** until they accept. What a new person sees when they first sign in is covered in [Sign in and two-factor](/docs/get-started/sign-in-and-security/).

## Remove someone

Disable the membership rather than deleting it. Their name stays on the approvals they gave and the evidence they collected, which is the point: an audit trail with holes in it is not an audit trail.

<Steps>
<Step title="Find the person">

Go to **Settings > Access Management > People** and open the row menu (⋯) for the person.

</Step>
<Step title="Disable the membership">

Choose **Disable member**, read the confirmation, and click **Disable membership**.

</Step>
</Steps>

They lose access to this workspace immediately. Their history stays in the audit trail, and you can invite them again later. A disabled person shows as **Disabled** in the list.

<Callout type="warning" title="Before you click">

Disabling takes effect at once: the person loses access to this workspace straight away. To give it back, invite them again. There is no delete, by design.

</Callout>

## A person, and a membership

A person has one sign-in, and a membership in each workspace they belong to. Every owner, approver and assignee in Verity points at a membership, so nobody can be attached to a workspace they do not belong to. Disabling a membership here affects only this workspace. See [Audit log and isolation](/docs/admin/audit-log-and-isolation/#data-isolation).
